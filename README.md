# Eliza Cesista

**CPA (Philippines) · US CMA · MBA**

Welcome to the professional portfolio of **Eliza Marie Cesista**, an accounting and finance professional with seven years of experience supporting reporting, compliance, close management, and finance-process improvement.

Eliza brings together the discipline of a Philippines CPA, the business perspective of a US Certified Management Accountant, and the leadership foundation of an MBA. Her work is grounded in a simple principle: reliable financial information helps leaders make timely, confident decisions.

## Professional Profile

Eliza has developed her career across audit, accounting advisory, and senior accounting roles. She has worked with teams and client operations in the Philippines and the UAE, including multi-currency consolidation for European client entities. Her experience combines technical accounting expertise with practical systems knowledge and a clear understanding of how finance teams operate day to day.

She currently serves as a Senior Accountant at NHBC Accounting & Bookkeeping LLC in Dubai, where she manages a multi-client portfolio across onboarding, full-cycle accounting, UAE VAT, corporate tax, reporting, and team mentorship.

Previously, at Arcadis Consulting Middle East Limited, Eliza progressed from General Accounting Analyst to General Accounting Specialist. She led close activities, improved Oracle NetSuite workflows, and collaborated with international stakeholders on reporting for European client entities. Earlier roles at ATC Healthcare International and PwC Philippines strengthened her experience in reconciliations, audit schedules, account closures, internal controls, substantive testing, and external audit procedures.
